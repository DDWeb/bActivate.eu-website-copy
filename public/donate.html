Does your mare qualify for a donated sample?

For breeds on a recognized conservation watchlist, we donate bActivate for the problem mares that will not get in foal. bActivate reveals a hidden Streptococcus zooepidemicus infection so your own veterinarian can treat it. Answer a few quick questions about your mare.

We donate bActivate for this mare

The bActivate itself is donated: your veterinarian receives a coupon and places the order in the bactivate.eu shop, and only the shipping is paid. Your own veterinarian handles her from the first culture to treatment. In return we ask only to record the outcome and, with your permission, to share the story if it succeeds.
